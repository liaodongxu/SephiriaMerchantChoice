# Third-party components

This repository distributes the Merchant Choice plugin source and its compiled plugin only.

- Sephiria game code, names, fonts, icons and UI sprites remain the property of their respective owners. The mod references the installed game's APIs and uses its assets at runtime; no decompiled game files, game assemblies or extracted art are distributed here.
- BepInEx, Harmony, Mono.Cecil, Unity, TextMeshPro and Mirror are external build/runtime dependencies, obtained from the user's own compatible installation. Their code and binaries are not bundled in this download, and remain under their respective licenses.
- The MIT license in this repository does not grant rights to those external components or to the game.

The mod was developed locally with AI coding assistance and extracted from a personal combined quality-of-life plugin. Its catalog eligibility checks are designed around the installed game's replenishment behavior. It is not a fork or repackaging of the backpack organizer, map revealer, reconnect or damage-meter mods.
